# Auth page images

These images are used as the split-screen brand panel on the sign in and
sign up pages (`src/pages/Login.jsx` and `src/pages/Register.jsx`).

- `auth-login.jpg` — "A Lawyer Behind His Desk" by Karola G., sourced from
  [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-lawyer-behind-his-desk-7876093/)
  (Pexels License — free for commercial use, no attribution required).
- `auth-register.jpg` — "Group of People on a Conference Room" by Christina
  Morillo, sourced from
  [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/group-of-people-on-a-conference-room-1181406/)
  (Pexels License — free for commercial use, no attribution required).

To swap in different images, replace the files (keep the same names) or
update the `src` paths in `Login.jsx` / `Register.jsx`.
